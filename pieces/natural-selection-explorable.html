Natural Selection Explorable

What this is

A native rebuild of the InquiryLabs peppered-moth explorable, "You Are the Predator" — no iframe, no dependency on the InquiryLabs app itself. You hunt a population of moths against the bark, breed whoever survives, and watch a camouflaged population assemble itself, generation by generation, out of nothing but which ones you happened to catch. Flip the habitat mid-run and watch selection reverse.

What this demonstrates

Designing interaction that surfaces a specific misconception — that organisms adapt on purpose — rather than just presenting it as content.
