Safe Isolation Procedure

A model of the safe sequence a licensed electrician follows to terminate an Australian double power point under AS/NZS 3000.

Your task

Complete this electrical termination according to the AS/NZS 3000 standard and leave the outlet in service.

  1. 1Click or tap switches to operate them.
  2. 2Drag and drop wires to their correct terminal positions.

Every action you take will be tracked. Press Finish & submit when you're done to see if you have completed this task safely and correctly.

This is a training model, not a how-to. In Australia, fixed electrical wiring must be carried out by a licensed electrician — doing it yourself is illegal and unsafe. Wiring colours and terminal markings follow AS/NZS 3000. No live work, and no electric shock, is ever depicted here.
